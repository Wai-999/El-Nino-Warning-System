# Production source registry

Verified against provider documentation and actual responses on 30 September 2026. This public-interest application is noncommercial and has no advertisements or subscriptions. All ingestion occurs in Node/GitHub Actions; browser CORS for third-party feeds is immaterial because clients fetch same-origin validated JSON. No API credentials are needed or shipped.

Sector context also uses [WHO drinking-water guidance](https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/drinking-water), [FAO El Niño](https://www.fao.org/el-nino/en/) and [WMO energy](https://wmo.int/themes/energy). These are general qualitative references, not operational measurement feeds; resolution/horizon/rate limits do not apply, content is linked and briefly paraphrased, and no full articles are redistributed.

## Evaluated but not ingested

[CHIRPS](https://www.chc.ucsb.edu/data/chirps) offers an independent rainfall product, but mixing its rainfall with an ERA5 baseline would be scientifically incompatible. A separate CHIRPS historical pipeline would be required. ERA5 is used consistently for V2. Soil moisture, vegetation, river/reservoir levels, disease, crop loss, grid outages and probabilistic rain forecasts are not inferred from missing feeds.

FAO/[GEOGLAM Crop Monitor](https://www.cropmonitor.org/)/NASA guidance and products were considered for future land monitoring; V2 agriculture signals are explicitly weather-based planning proxies, not satellite crop assessments. DMH remains a prominent official-source link; no reliable machine-readable warning feed was verified. WMO, ReliefWeb/OCHA, GDACS and AHA Centre remain candidate warning/update providers; none is treated as complete Myanmar official-warning coverage. The current automatically curated updates feed is limited to validated NOAA publications. It is labeled global ENSO context, not a Myanmar emergency-news service.

## Provenance and use constraints

Forecasts and reanalysis use `elevation=nan`, `cell_selection=nearest`, consistent coordinates and Asia/Yangon days. Grid snapping is recorded and validated. The boundary aggregation and three-cell stratified sampling are modified/derived products. Public values are rounded to useful precision; the calculation preserves unrounded intermediate values. Original source links accompany each dataset. Provider failure never changes valid time. A future commercial operator must obtain an appropriate Open-Meteo plan before using the free endpoint commercially. See [Open-Meteo terms](https://open-meteo.com/en/terms) and [weighted-call pricing documentation](https://open-meteo.com/en/pricing).

## Impacts explanatory evidence (verified 1 October 2026)

- [NOAA PMEL: What is El Niño?](https://www.pmel.noaa.gov/elnino/what-is-el-nino) supports weakened trade winds, shifted tropical rainfall and atmospheric circulation. Its examples include 1982–83 and 1997–98; an original publication date is not stated.
- [NOAA CPC: El Niño temperature and precipitation patterns](https://www.cpc.ncep.noaa.gov/products/analysis_monitoring/ensocycle/elninosfc_body.html) supports general global and Asian seasonal relationships. It provides neither a Myanmar-specific current forecast nor explicit composite years/graded confidence.
- [FAO March 2016 El Niño response report](https://www.fao.org/fileadmin/user_upload/emergencies/docs/FAOEl%20NinoReportMarch2016.pdf#page=38), Myanmar entry, PDF p. 38 / printed p. 35, supplies a dated case: drier conditions since November, water-shortage concerns, and recovery from 2015 floods. It is not treated as proof of ENSO attribution for every local event, a damage forecast, or a calibrated regional probability.
- [WHO ENSO fact sheet](<https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/el-nino-southern-oscillation-(enso)>), 9 November 2023, supports conditional health pathways, other vulnerability factors and fire/smoke context. The app does not infer Myanmar disease incidence or wildfire/haze conditions.
- [WHO heat and health](https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/climate-change-heat-and-health), displayed date 31 July 2026; [WHO drinking-water](https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/drinking-water), 13 September 2023; [FAO El Niño](https://www.fao.org/el-nino/en/); and [WMO Energy](https://wmo.int/themes/energy) support general sector mechanisms and preparedness. These are attributed international sources. Platform suggestions are not presented as Myanmar government orders.

Publication dates are source metadata; the verification date denotes editorial review, not an updated climate observation. The structured registry is `src/data/impactEvidence.ts`. Burmese and English explanatory text are provided, while original source titles are preserved for traceability.

## 2.1 reusable registry and additional context

The reusable operational registry is now `src/data/sources.ts`; `sourceStatus()` joins dataset periods, retrieval/review dates, source-specific freshness and fetch failure metadata. It records tier, organization, dataset, URL, geographic/temporal coverage, cadence, license, method and limitations. The existing impacts explanatory registry remains a companion for detailed sector claims. Both are source-backed; they are not interchangeable warning feeds.

New selected context: [ASMC seasonal outlook](https://asmc.asean.org/asmc-seasonal-outlook/) (tier 2, September–November 2026 issue); [FAO GIEWS Myanmar country brief](https://www.fao.org/giews/countrybrief/country.jsp?code=MMR&lang=en) (tier 3, 18 September 2026 national crop calendar and explicitly named historical impact areas); [WHO heat and health](https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/climate-change-heat-and-health) (tier 3, general guidance, reviewed 2 October 2026). No source images or full articles are redistributed; only short original paraphrases and links. ASMC is always labeled REGIONAL CONTEXT — NOT A MYANMAR OFFICIAL WARNING. FAO historical records retain a date-specific excerpt in the app because the country brief URL is a living page; its [PDF archive](https://www.fao.org/giews/countrybrief/country/MMR/pdf_archive/MMR_Archive.pdf) is an additional audit reference.

The [official administrative list](https://myanmarbsb.org/_site/states-and-regions/) independently confirms the 15 canonical units. Display names retain MIMU transliterations; source aliases handle name variants without fuzzy matching. No neighboring-country warnings are ingested.

DMH's unverified feed remains unavailable, not an all-clear. Drought, flood, local water storage, crop stage, disease and fire/haze operational feeds remain gaps. Candidate SPI/SPEI, soil moisture, vegetation, river, IOD/MJO and crop data decisions, source access observations and fallback rules are detailed in [WARNING_SYSTEM_RESEARCH.md](../WARNING_SYSTEM_RESEARCH.md).

## 2.2 health, media and source completion review

See the [clinical source matrix](../HEALTH_GUIDANCE_METHODOLOGY.md) for WHO, CDC and NHS sources and publication/review dates; [data gaps](../DATA_GAPS.md) records the structured-source quality gate and climate foundation review. Medical guidance is summarized with attribution and links, not bulk-rehosted source text or images. The dated WHO Health Cluster August report adds qualitative location-qualified surveillance context; it is not a real-time feed and does not count as current regional incidence in the coverage model.

The two BBC Burmese video identities, metadata dates and durations are retained in `src/data/videos.ts`. YouTube player/oEmbed/channel metadata verified their provenance. Media embedding and redistribution rights were not assumed: playback stays on YouTube, thumbnails load from the publisher/platform's public image URL only after user interaction, and no downloaded video or thumbnail is packaged for offline redistribution. Future link rot remains possible. Full transcripts were unavailable; review scope is disclosed in the UI.

No new operational API, key, paid source or numerical warning feed was connected in 2.2. Existing ECMWF/ERA5/NOAA ingestion, schema validation, period preservation and failure logs continue unchanged. WMO GSCU, CPC RONI, GloFAS, FAO ASIS and NASA FIRMS were evaluated and linked with explicit integration limits. No source access failure was converted to an all-clear.
