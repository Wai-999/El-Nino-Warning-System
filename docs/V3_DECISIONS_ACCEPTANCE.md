## Implementation plan

- Version the MIMU/OCHA township gazetteer with its true geographic vintage, parent hierarchy, codes, license and checksum. Validate every parent and code. Administrative lookup does not create township-scale weather or flood measurements.
- Add a simple bilingual township selector, reviewed aliases, overview, multiple device-local saved places and explicit per-indicator parent-region fallback. Never silently widen a township bulletin to its region.
- Default to international numerals, offer Myanmar numerals, retain Myanmar time and language. Add a bilingual terminology glossary; expert review remains a separately identified human gate.
- Put weather and an accessible map beside each other on desktop; use lightweight weather animation with pause, reduced-motion and low-data behavior.
- Generate a deterministic bilingual local briefing from validated dated inputs. Keep missing data unknown and show source times.
- Replace the static clinical-health panel with independently fetched official WHO health bulletin metadata. A bulletin without an expiry cannot become an active warning. Country taxonomy, issue dates and coverage limits must survive caching and failures.
- Retain the explicit subscription blocker until a verified warning feed and secure tested delivery infrastructure exist. No fake toggles or emergency-delivery guarantee.
- Run required checks, inspect responsive UI, publish and verify the production source/data chain.

## Verification and remaining external gates

- `tests/v3-decisions.test.ts`: original geographic parents/code collisions, duplicate/unknown code rejection, aliases, ambiguous names, country-tag false positives, CMS versus source issue dates, unknown expiry, safe URLs, future/duplicate data, empty healthy source, dated failure cache and missing briefing inputs.
- `tests/e2e/v3-decisions.spec.ts`: township persistence and accessible overview, historical names, ambiguous choices, numeral/motion preferences, per-place checklist/crop separation, old WHO issues after new retrieval, responsive map/briefing and unavailable states. Existing scientific, offline, upgrade, QR and bilingual WCAG suites pass.
- Manual browser review confirmed Burmese township search and the Kachin parent-region label after choosing Myitkyina. Screenshots in `docs/qa/` show the actual updated UI. This is not expert Burmese-reader certification.
- Original research and exact endpoints: `docs/research/V3_GEOGRAPHY_SOURCES.md`, `docs/research/V3_HEALTH_SOURCES.md`; correction procedure in `docs/GEOGRAPHY_MAINTENANCE.md`.
- Direct official Myanmar warning/health coverage, complete village geography, township flood/gauge products, calibrated advanced climate indicators, delivery infrastructure and independent human/device review cannot be declared complete without the evidence described above. Every such capability remains labeled with its limitation rather than substituted with fabricated data.
