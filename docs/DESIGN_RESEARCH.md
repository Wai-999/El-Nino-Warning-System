# Design, source and cost research

Primary sources checked 31 October 2026. These are design and integration decisions, not authorization to copy provider branding or graphics.

## Public-service design evidence

| Evidence                                                                                                                                                                          | Consequence for this app                                                                                                                                                                                |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [WMO Common Alerting Protocol](https://wmo.int/site/wmo-common-alerting-protocol)                                                                                                 | Show issuer, hazard, affected area, original severity, publication and validity, evidence and protective actions. Do not replace the issuer's taxonomy with platform screening levels.                  |
| [Met Office warning guide](https://weather.metoffice.gov.uk/guides/warnings)                                                                                                      | Put the headline, impacts and practical actions before scientific detail. Do not import UK warning thresholds into Myanmar. The guide's matrix terminology changed in October 2026.                     |
| [NOAA monthly and seasonal outlook explanation](https://content-drupal.climate.gov/news-features/understanding-climate/understanding-noaas-monthly-and-seasonal-climate-outlooks) | Show probabilities and valid periods; seasonal likelihood is different from a daily forecast.                                                                                                           |
| [WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/)                                                                                                                                         | Visible keyboard focus, unobscured controls, sufficient contrast, adequate targets, semantic headings and list/table alternatives to graphics. Automated testing is not full conformance certification. |
| [NOAA ENSO explanation](https://oceanservice.noaa.gov/facts/ninonina.html)                                                                                                        | Use a lightweight conceptual ocean/wind diagram with explicit synthetic labeling. Do not imply deterministic Myanmar rainfall.                                                                          |

Design inference: a calm white-first shell, a dominant dated weather block, a short ENSO context panel, limited homepage content and expandable technical material help readers act without interpreting an entire dashboard. Top navigation on desktop and bottom navigation on mobile keep the core pages discoverable. Self-hosted Noto Sans Myanmar avoids a third-party font dependency. Custom Myanmar dates/digits handle browsers lacking a native Burmese locale.

## ENSO methodology verified

[NOAA's operational notice](https://www.weather.gov/media/notification/pdf_2026/pns26-05_Relative_ONI.pdf) made RONI operational on 1 February 2026. The [current methodology](https://www.cpc.ncep.noaa.gov/products/analysis_monitoring/enso/roni/) uses ERSSTv6 and the 1991–2020 baseline: relative Niño 3.4 removes tropical-mean anomalies, adjusts variance and takes three-month means. Newest values may be revised for two months. Historical event coloring additionally requires consecutive seasons; the app does not classify a local hazard from an index threshold.

The [raw RONI table](https://www.cpc.ncep.noaa.gov/data/indices/RONI.ascii.txt) supplies seasons from 1950 onward, without a precise publication timestamp. The [phase probability table](https://www.cpc.ncep.noaa.gov/products/analysis_monitoring/enso/roni/probabilities/) supplies nine overlapping seasons and an issue month, not a clock time. Both are independently parsed and validated. The [diagnostic discussion](https://www.cpc.ncep.noaa.gov/products/analysis_monitoring/enso_advisory/ensodisc.shtml), phase probabilities, strength statement and monthly Niño 3.4 series remain separate quantities.

## Static safety and agriculture

Use short paraphrases and original links: [WHO heat prevention](https://www.who.int/news-room/questions-and-answers/item/heatwaves-how-to-stay-cool), [WHO heat/health](https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/climate-change-heat-and-health), [WHO flood health](https://www.who.int/news-room/questions-and-answers/item/how-do-i-protect-my-health-in-a-flood), [NWS flood safety](https://www.weather.gov/safety/flood-during), [NWS before wind](https://www.weather.gov/safety/wind-before), [NWS during wind](https://www.weather.gov/safety/wind-during). Health material is static education, independent of weather inputs; weather does not establish disease transmission.

[FAO GIEWS Myanmar](https://www.fao.org/giews/countrybrief/country.jsp?code=MMR), [Myanmar crop context](https://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/mya190978.pdf) and [crop water guidance](https://www.fao.org/4/S2022E/s2022e07.htm) support starting with rice, maize and pulses. Field water adequacy, soil moisture, local species and growth stage remain unknown. The selectors change general considerations, not sowing dates or yield predictions.

## Zero-cost architecture comparison

| Option                                           | Current limits / cost evidence                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   | Risk / decision                                                                                                       |
| ------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Static Vercel Hobby + scheduled shared snapshots | [Hobby](https://vercel.com/docs/plans/hobby): personal/noncommercial only; 100 GB fast transfer, 10 GB origin, 1M CDN requests/month. [Limits](https://vercel.com/docs/limits): 100 deployments/day; 45-minute builds.                                                                                                           | Selected. Four bounded scheduled data commits daily fit deployment count; monitor bandwidth. No paid service enabled. |
| Vercel Functions / cron                          | 1M invocations and 4 CPU-hours/month on Hobby. [Cron](https://vercel.com/docs/cron-jobs/usage-and-pricing): 100 jobs/project, each Hobby job at most daily and imprecise within the hour.                                                                                                                                        | No cron/functions configured. Daily jobs cannot guarantee immediate warnings.                                         |
| Browser fetches every provider                   | Calls grow with visitors; provider failure/limits vary by client.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                | Rejected as primary path; use validated same-origin shared snapshots.                                                 |
| GitHub scheduled ingestion                       | [Public-repository standard runners are free](https://docs.github.com/en/billing/concepts/product-billing/github-actions). [Schedules](https://docs.github.com/en/actions/reference/workflows-and-actions/events-that-trigger-workflows#schedule) may delay/drop, run on default branch and disable after 60 days of inactivity. | Retain four daily schedules away from minute zero and manual dispatch. Never claim emergency-grade delivery.          |

[Vercel Vite support](https://vercel.com/docs/frameworks/frontend/vite) permits retaining this framework. Hash routing needs no catch-all rewrite that could mask missing JSON with HTML. Longer term, a separate snapshot publishing endpoint could avoid even four data-triggered builds daily; no new storage service is justified at current scale.
