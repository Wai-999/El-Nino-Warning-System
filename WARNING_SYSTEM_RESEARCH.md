# Warning system research and source decisions

Reviewed 2 October 2026 UTC. This is a preparedness screening service, not a warning authority. The reusable registry is `src/data/sources.ts`; runtime timestamps and source health are joined from the validated source snapshots, never replaced with build time.

## Source hierarchy and geographic safeguards

1. Myanmar authorities: DMH is the preferred meteorological/hydrological warning source. Its root URLs were inaccessible through the research browser in this audit; no stable machine feed, reuse license or current bulletin was verified. Official coverage remains unavailable. Health, agriculture and water authority feeds also remain unconnected. This does not mean no warnings exist.
2. ASEAN: [ASMC seasonal outlook](https://asmc.asean.org/asmc-seasonal-outlook/) is genuine Southeast Asia context. September–November 2026 outlook was issued 2 September. Mainland rainfall agreement is limited; temperatures are generally favoured above normal. Do not convert the map into Myanmar state probabilities. [Terms](https://asmc.asean.org/terms-of-use/) checked; only a short paraphrase and link are included. Monthly review, aging at 35 days, stale at 62 days or validity end, whichever comes first.
3. International operational: existing ECMWF IFS forecast and ERA5 reanalysis through Open-Meteo, NOAA CPC assessment/monthly SST; add dated FAO crop calendar and WHO guidance as context, not measured regional risk.
4. Research: mechanisms and potential indices can inform methodology, but no research paper is ingested as a live alert. WMO's [SPI guide](https://www.droughtmanagement.info/literature/WMO_standardized_precipitation_index_user_guide_en_2012.pdf), [ECMWF hydrological forecasting](https://www.ecmwf.int/en/research/projects/efas), and [BOM MJO explanation](https://www.bom.gov.au/climate/mjo/index.shtml) informed candidate screening. Operational tiers do not confer Myanmar issuing authority on international publishers.

The [official Myanmar administrative listing](https://myanmarbsb.org/_site/states-and-regions/) confirms seven states, seven regions and one Union Territory. [MIMU/OCHA COD-AB](https://data.humdata.org/dataset/cod-ab-mmr) supplies actual boundaries (CC BY 3.0 IGO); Bago and Shan subdivisions are combined only for geometry. Unknown aliases fail validation; subregion alerts must not be expanded to their parent administrative unit.

## Researched but not integrated as live hazards

| Candidate                           | Decision value                          | Why deferred / explicit coverage gap                                                                                                                                                                              |
| ----------------------------------- | --------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| SPI/SPEI                            | Persistent meteorological drought       | A valid fitted multidecadal precipitation (and for SPEI water-balance) series is required; existing 30-day values are not such an index. No arbitrary conversion.                                                 |
| Soil moisture                       | Root-zone water availability            | ERA5 layers are model dependent; crop rooting depths and a validated local baseline/threshold are absent.                                                                                                         |
| NDVI / vegetation stress / FAO ASIS | Crop condition                          | [FAO Earth Observation](https://www.fao.org/giews/earthobservation/) is promising; seasonal masks, dekadal ingestion, quality flags and admin aggregation have not been validated for this application.           |
| Flood / river / flash flood         | Immediate protective decisions          | Prefer DMH river/bulletin products; verified feed unavailable. GloFAS uses hydrological models and ensemble forecasts, not rainfall totals alone. River network matching and calibration are not yet implemented. |
| Fire / haze                         | Respiratory exposure, fire preparedness | ASMC hotspots are not ground PM2.5 or proof of fire exposure in a Myanmar region. No defensible regional live classification added.                                                                               |
| Water availability                  | Water supply planning                   | No validated reservoir, streamflow or supply observations. Rainfall proxy remains explicitly limited.                                                                                                             |
| IOD / MJO                           | Additional climate background           | Temporal/spatial influence needs skill assessment for Myanmar; Australian impacts cannot be transplanted. No local severity adjustment or arbitrary combined score.                                               |
| Crop loss / disease outbreak        | Impact response                         | No local exposure, stage, disease surveillance or confounder model; unavailable rather than fabricated.                                                                                                           |

## Coverage and fallback policy

Expected coverage is eight explicitly named categories for each of 15 units (120 cells): temperature reanalysis, precipitation reanalysis, forecast, drought assessment, flood assessment, agriculture context, health guidance, official advisory coverage. Agriculture context and health guidance are qualitative national/general sources, not local observations; the KPI explicitly separates their 30 possible cells from 90 monitoring cells. No percentage is called a risk score.

Only current/aging validated evidence counts as usable. Stale evidence stays dated and excluded. Failures retain last valid provider data with original issue/validity and explicit failed status. No automatic provider/model substitution, no baseline mixing. A failed source can still have usable dated retained evidence; failure counts are separate from freshness counts. Static context review timestamps are never refreshed by a scheduled weather fetch.

Official warnings must be issued by the connected Myanmar authority registry entry (`dmh`), country `MM`, with source-described location, canonical ID and valid interval. Regional/international evidence uses context channels and cannot pass this schema. No foreign warning parser or fallback exists.

Historical records retain their publication and event periods and geographic limitations. Missing categories (especially Myanmar-specific heat, health and fire event archives) remain visible as gaps. Historical ENSO context does not establish causality or forecast recurrence.

## History / scheduled architecture

GitHub Actions keeps the existing four daily scheduled checks (04:17, 10:17, 16:17, 22:17 UTC). Full original data snapshots remain in Git. A bounded archive of up to 124 validated compact summaries supports at least roughly one month at the regular cadence; extra manual releases shorten that window. Existing bulletin history is retained up to 365 days in the archive. A fresh baseline starts with this release; no historical pipeline state is invented. Changes only compare matching methods and actual independent hazard levels, with data loss/restoration distinguished from increased/decreased concern. Forecast updates compare different forecast windows and are not observed trends. Pipeline timing is an expected schedule, not a guaranteed publication time.
