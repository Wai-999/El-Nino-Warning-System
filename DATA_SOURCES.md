# Data sources

The maintained [source and licensing registry](docs/DATA_SOURCES.md) describes provider metadata, selected datasets and limitations. The [upgrade research](WARNING_SYSTEM_RESEARCH.md) records candidates, decisions and known gaps. Runtime registry: `src/data/sources.ts`; original retrieval/data dates come from validated snapshots, not build times.

Version 2.2 source evaluations, rejected candidates, access/geography limitations and review targets are recorded in [DATA_GAPS.md](DATA_GAPS.md). The clinical reference matrix and official Burmese resources are in [HEALTH_GUIDANCE_METHODOLOGY.md](HEALTH_GUIDANCE_METHODOLOGY.md). Source metadata lives in `src/data/healthGuidance.ts`, qualitative surveillance in `src/data/surveillance.ts`, and reviewed media identities in `src/data/videos.ts`. News videos are educational references, never primary operational or medical evidence.
